Presenting a sleep-pattern analysis at KidoCode

Mohammed Safwanul Islam AI & Product Engineer @ PreCognise · Oct 31, 2024

1 min read

I just gave a presentation with my friend Arsyi Aditama. As interns at KidoCode, we are open to learning and understanding things outside the box. It's not only about teaching kids; it's also about growing our own programming knowledge for the future.

The project

Today we presented a project on a dataset from kaggle.com about the sleep pattern cycles of people aged 18 to 25. We walked through the data to show the other interns how descriptive analysis actually works in data science.

Our supervisor, Farhad Hossain, helped us sharpen how to present a descriptive analysis more effectively.
