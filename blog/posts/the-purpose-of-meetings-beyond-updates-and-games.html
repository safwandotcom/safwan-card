The purpose of meetings: beyond updates and games

Oct 9, 2024 2 min read

Meetings are essential tools for effective collaboration and problem-solving within teams. While they often involve sharing updates and providing feedback, their true value lies in fostering a shared understanding of goals, promoting teamwork, and driving progress.

Why meetings matter

  • Information sharing. Meetings give team members a place to share updates on their work, discuss challenges, and ask for guidance. This keeps everyone aligned on goals and progress.
  • Decision making. Group discussion lets a team analyze information together, weigh options, and make informed decisions, drawing on everyone's different perspectives.
  • Problem solving. Bringing together people with different skills and experiences stimulates creative problem-solving. Teams can brainstorm solutions, identify root causes, and develop action plans.
  • Building relationships. Regular meetings build a positive team culture by fostering trust, camaraderie, and a sense of belonging, which improves collaboration and productivity.
  • Accountability. By setting clear expectations and tracking progress, meetings help team members own their responsibilities and stay focused and motivated.

Games are a means, not the goal

Activities like games and icebreakers can be valuable for building team cohesion, but the primary purpose of a meeting should be to achieve these core objectives. Run well, meetings help teams maximize productivity, improve communication, and reach their goals.
