Scholarship training for UCSI staff and student ambassadors

Oct 23, 2024 1 min read

The Student Enrolment Center (SEC) at UCSI recently held a training session for new staff, counsellors and student ambassadors on how to better promote scholarships.

Most of the training was conducted by our Higher Education Counsellor at SEC, Ms Jasvin Vining. It also covered sales techniques and how to talk effectively with prospective students and parents about scholarships, led by Yogishraao Bemaraju.

The training helped everyone learn new things about sales and become better at their jobs. A big thanks to our boss, Parameswari Karunganam, for arranging these trainings for the student ambassadors.
